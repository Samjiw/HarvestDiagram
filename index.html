Section 01 / Technical Reference
3D Model Specification
Overview: HARVEST SOS is a wrist-worn device in two integrated parts — the BASE BAND (energy harvesting) and the SOS POD (electronics + beacon module). The pod attaches to the band via a standard 24 mm lug system with spring bars. When assembled, it resembles a chunky sport smartwatch. All dimensions below are precise — use them as absolute targets for the 3D model.

Base Band — Geometry
Band width
26 mm (constant across full length)
Clasp arm length
120 mm (longer arm — wraps over wrist)
Buckle arm length
80 mm (shorter arm — holds buckle end)
Band thickness (std)
4.5 mm (most of band length)
EM coil housing
Thickened section on clasp arm, 35 mm from lug end. Circular raised section ∅ 22 mm × 8.5 mm (total thickness in this zone = 12 mm). Smooth bump, not angular.
Lug transition
Band width narrows from 26 mm to 24 mm over a 6 mm taper section at each lug end.
Inner surface contour
Concave lengthwise (R ≈ 80 mm arc) to fit wrist. Two raised TEG contact pads (see below).
Material (band body)
Black fluoroelastomer (FKM rubber), Shore 60A hardness. UV and sweat resistant.
Clasp
Deployant (butterfly) style, 304 stainless steel, brushed finish. Folded thickness ≈ 5 mm. Width matches band (26 mm). Attaches at end of buckle arm.
Base Band — Surface Features
PV cells (outer surface)
12 flexible GaAs cells in a 3-column × 4-row grid. Each cell: 8 mm × 14 mm, 1 mm gap between cells. Grid starts 8 mm from lug end, runs 72 mm along clasp arm. Cells are 0.2 mm proud of band surface. Dark blue-grey colour.
TEG contact pads (inner)
2 raised metal pads (copper-nickel alloy), each 16 mm × 20 mm, 0.6 mm proud of inner surface. Positioned symmetrically: centres at 30 mm and 70 mm from lug end of clasp arm, centred across band width. Smooth and polished (skin contact).
Piezo ridges (inner)
2 transverse ridges across the inner surface, 1.2 mm tall × 3 mm wide × 18 mm long. Embedded piezo film beneath. Positions: 50 mm and 80 mm from lug end of clasp arm. Ridges flex with wrist movement.
Electrical channel
Two 1.5 mm × 1 mm channels run along each side edge of the band (left and right edges, full length). Gold-plated copper conductors embedded — carry power from harvesters to lug spring contacts.
Ventilation
5 pairs of 2 mm × 6 mm elongated slots on inner surface, evenly spaced on buckle arm, to reduce heat buildup and improve TEG ΔT.

Full System — Electrical Block Diagram
How to read this schematic: Power flows left → right (red lines). The STM32L0 sits below the main power path and sends control signals upward (blue dashed) to each N-FET gate, turning off any source below its threshold voltage. It also sends data/SPI/UART signals rightward (green dashed) to command the LoRa radio and GPS. The supercapacitor is in parallel with the LiPo and absorbs LoRa TX current spikes (~120 mA for 10ms) that the battery alone could not cleanly supply. The rescue receiver is a separate hardware unit at the incident command post — NOT part of the wearable.
